# Persona Run-Throughs

The following run-throughs demonstrate how the Borrower Copilot mathematically handles the three personas outlined in the brief. These are based on exact outputs generated by the application.

---

## 1. Priya (Prime Salaried, Overpaying)

**Profile:** ₹1.1L/mo salaried, 5 years at job, ₹28k expenses, ₹14k existing EMI. Wants ₹8L for a wedding. 780 Credit Score. Age 30.

### Questions Asked & Adaptive Logic
*   **Must-questions asked:** All basic fields (Purpose, Amount Wanted, Quoted Rate, Tenure, Age, Income, Expenses, Existing EMIs, Credit Score).
*   **Questions Adaptively Skipped:** 
    *   *ITR & Variable Income %:* Skipped. **Why:** She selected "Salaried (Payslip)". The engine trusts her declared salary and does not require informal business checks.
    *   *Property Pledge/Value:* Skipped. **Why:** She did not check "I own unencumbered property".

### The Four Explicit Outputs
1.  **Output 1: Verdict**
    *   **Result:** Safe to Borrow
    *   **Why:** The requested amount fits well within your disposable income and survives our macroeconomic stress test.
2.  **Output 2: Two Amounts**
    *   **What a Bank Will Sanction:** ₹15,70,862 - ₹19,82,310 
    *   **What is Actually Safe For You:** ₹10,91,489 - ₹14,02,937 
    *   **Why this gap exists:** Banks look at maximum theoretical affordability (FOIR), while we calculate your actual survival limit after living expenses and stress tests.
3.  **Output 3: Fair Rate + APR**
    *   **Fair Interest Rate:** 10.5% - 11.5%
    *   **Why:** True APR is ~10.9% - 11.9% (assuming 2% processing fee).
4.  **Output 4: EMI Ceiling + Stress Case**
    *   **Safe Monthly EMI Ceiling:** ₹30,000
    *   **Why:** Stress Test: If income drops 10% and rates rise 2%, your EMI becomes ₹17,869. You will have ₹39,131 left over for living expenses.

### Full Negotiation Card Text
*   **Target Product:** Personal Loan
*   **Quoted Rate Check:** The lender quoted you 14%. This is higher than our fair band. You are overpaying and should negotiate.
*   **Ask The Lender For:**
    *   Is the interest rate reducing-balance or flat-rate?
    *   What is the exact processing fee %?
    *   Are there pre-payment penalties if I clear it early?
*   **Walk Away If:**
    *   An EMI higher than ₹30,000
    *   A rate higher than 12.5%
    *   Any upfront fees not deducted from the principal

## 2. Ravi (Asset Rich, Missing Info, Co-Applicant)

**Profile:** ₹60k/mo (informal business), Wife earns ₹18k (salaried). ITR shows ₹4.2L. Owns a ₹45L house unencumbered. Never taken a formal loan (NTC). Wants ₹15L. Age 45. Leaves expenses blank.

### Questions Asked & Adaptive Logic
*   **Must-questions asked:** Basic fields (Purpose, Amount Wanted, Tenure, Age, Income, Co-Applicant Income, Credit Status).
*   **Questions Adaptively Surfaced:** 
    *   *Co-Applicant Salaried Checkbox:* Surfaced. **Why:** He entered a co-applicant income > 0. The engine asks if they are salaried to exempt their income from the business volatility haircut.
    *   *Variable Income % & ITR:* Surfaced. **Why:** He selected "Self-Employed / Informal", triggering the informal income assessment module.
    *   *Property Pledge/Value:* Surfaced. **Why:** He checked that he owns property. The engine asks if he will pledge it to drastically lower his rate.
*   **Questions Skipped by User:**
    *   *Household Expenses / Existing EMIs:* Left blank. **Why:** Modeled his reluctance to share full data. The engine heavily widens the safety band as a penalty.

### The Four Explicit Outputs
1.  **Output 1: Verdict**
    *   **Result:** Borrow Less
    *   **Why:** The amount you want (₹15,00,000) exceeds what you can survive under a stress test (₹9,72,551). Borrow a smaller amount to stay safe.
2.  **Output 2: Two Amounts**
    *   **What a Bank Will Sanction:** ₹0 - ₹15,31,093
    *   **What is Actually Safe For You:** ₹0 - ₹9,72,551
    *   **Why this gap exists:** The floor drops to zero because of massive uncertainty widening (skipped expenses).
3.  **Output 3: Fair Rate + APR**
    *   **Fair Interest Rate:** 9.0% - 10.0%
    *   **Why:** True APR is ~9.4% - 10.4% (assuming 2% processing fee).
4.  **Output 4: EMI Ceiling + Stress Case**
    *   **Safe Monthly EMI Ceiling:** ₹20,175
    *   **Why:** Stress Test: If income drops 10% and rates rise 2%, your EMI becomes ₹21,304. You will have ₹5,296 left over for living expenses.

### Full Negotiation Card Text
*   **Target Product:** Loan Against Property (LAP)
*   **Why:** We routed you away from a Personal Loan to match what you actually qualify for based on your collateral.
*   **Ask The Lender For:**
    *   Is the interest rate reducing-balance or flat-rate?
    *   What is the exact processing fee %?
    *   Are there pre-payment penalties if I clear it early?
*   **Walk Away If:**
    *   An EMI higher than ₹20,175
    *   A rate higher than 11.0%
    *   Any upfront fees not deducted from the principal
